# Roadmap

This roadmap is built only from [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md) and [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md). It has no dates and promises nothing beyond them.

## Now

What the latest release, 0.3.0, already does:

- Sweeps job sources, filters and deduplicates in code.
- Builds CVs only from verified facts and lints them.
- Assists form filling in your own browser and stops before Submit.
- Syncs replies from your inbox.
- Tracks status as a fold over the event log, with the Guard denying Submit and Send.

Nothing else is in progress yet.

## Next

Nothing committed yet.

## Later

Nothing committed yet. The [non-goals in PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md#non-goals) stay out of scope: auto-applying or auto-sending, a hosted service, and claims the Fact bank cannot back.
