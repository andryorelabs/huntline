Voice

jobship speaks like a precise, calm colleague who shows the work and leaves the decision to you.

Search less. Decide better.

Even a small chance of moving forward takes many applications, and nobody can read every Posting by hand all day, so jobship does the analysis from your Profile and the data you give it, and you make the final decision.

We are We are not
Precise: one claim, one fact, one file that backs it Hype: no superlatives, no "revolutionary"
Calm: the search is hard enough without pressure Urgent: no fear of the market, no countdowns
Trustworthy: we claim no more than the CLI does today Promising: no interviews, offers or hit rates
Plain: short sentences and the glossary's words Clever: no puns, no jargon without a gloss
On the human's side: you decide and you press Submit Autonomous: the tool never applies or sends
Auditable: every claim can be read back from the event log Magic: nothing happens that code cannot show

Avoid

Five things the brand leaves out, from the brief.

Words
"AI magic", "auto-apply", "autonomous", "supercharge", "land your dream job", "while you sleep", and any hunting or weapon language.
Imagery
hunting and weapon imagery such as crosshairs, targets, arrows and traps.
Colour
Claude orange or clay, sparks and starbursts, LinkedIn blue, and purple.
Claims
speed, hit-rate or outcome claims, and anything the Fact bank or the code cannot back.
Tone
pressure, urgency or fear about the job market.
