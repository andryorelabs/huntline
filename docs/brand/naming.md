# Naming

How the name was chosen. The pick lives in the decisions table of [README.md](README.md).

## Path

1. Rounds 1 to 5 ([naming-candidates.md](naming-candidates.md)) produced finalists that the maintainer rejected: no job link, too compound, not memorable, Spanish.
2. [naming-research.md](naming-research.md) studied how professionals name a product and produced eight territories. The maintainer picked **D, the craft of getting hired**.
3. Round 6 generated 127 names inside that territory and scored eight finalists out of 80.
4. The maintainer picked `jobhood`, then replaced it with `jobship` because "hood" reads as Robin Hood.

## Rules the name had to meet

One English word, friendly, memorable, with a job cue, free on npm and GitHub, lowercase and typeable as a CLI command and as `/<name>:sweep`.
